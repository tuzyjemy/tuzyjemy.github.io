Before submitting the package of three documents, we conducted a public collection
online signatures. We collected signatures until **on June 23, 2026**, and then we added
them to the documents forwarded to the addressees.

## How many signatures

**3 770 signatures** - as of August 24, 2026

The counter on the collection page is updated on an ongoing basis; the number given is the reading from this
day, not a value automatically converted on our website.
