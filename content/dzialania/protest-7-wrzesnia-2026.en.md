An assembly outside the Department for Foreigners of the Pomeranian Voivodeship Office,
ul. Chmielna 74/76 in Gdańsk, on 7 September 2026, from 16:30 to 18:00. Notified and
entered in the register of public assemblies of the Gdańsk City Office.

**Between 200 and 300 people** came — more than had confirmed their attendance.

## What we handed over

We handed the petition prepared for the protest to the first vice-voivode, Emil Rojek.
It contained **11 points** from [the protest demands](/postulaty/) — those falling within
the competence of the office. The remaining 4 concern nationwide matters and are addressed
to the government and the Sejm.

[The petition of 7 September 2026 — details and status](/dzialania/petycja-wojewoda-wrzesien-2026/)

## What was agreed

- The vice-voivode promised **24 new posts** in the department for foreigners.
- A **working meeting** with the office was agreed.

A promise of posts changes nothing by itself. We will ask what kind of posts these will be
— people who conduct cases and issue decisions, or front-desk service — when they start
work, and how that shows up in the waiting times.

## Media coverage

The protest was reported by TVP Gdańsk, Dziennik Bałtycki, Gazeta Prawna and Puls Gdańska,
among others.
