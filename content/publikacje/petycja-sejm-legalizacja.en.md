Petition **regarding taking a legislative initiative regarding the legalization of stay
foreigners** was submitted to the Sejm on June 24, 2026.

## Mileage

| Date | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 24/06/2026 | Petition submitted to the Sejm |
| 15/07/2026 | Referral to the Petitions Committee |

The further course of the matter depends on the agenda of the Commission.


{{< notatka typ="info" >}}
This is one of **three documents submitted on June 24, 2026.** as a common package of actions to improve the legalization of stay. The initiative was preceded by a public collection of signatures. The other two documents: [the petition to the Pomeranian Voivode](/publikacje/petycja-wojewoda-pomorski/) and [the letter on digitalisation](/publikacje/wystapienie-cyfryzacja/).
