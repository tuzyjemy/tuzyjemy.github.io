Petition **regarding taking a legislative initiative regarding the legalization of stay
foreigners** was submitted to the Sejm on June 24, 2026.

## Mileage

| Date | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 24/06/2026 | Petition submitted to the Sejm |
| 15/07/2026 | Referral to the Petitions Committee |
| 24.09.2026 | Extension of the consideration deadline (Przedłużenie terminu) |

The further course of the matter depends on the agenda of the Commission.

### Update of 27 September 2026

The [official petition record](https://www.sejm.gov.pl/sejm10.nsf/agent.xsp?symbol=PETYCJA&NrPetycji=BKSP-153-X-1098%2F26) added **“Przedłużenie terminu” — extension of the deadline** on 24 September. As of 27 September, no new end date is shown. The entry does not report a substantive decision on the demands. We continue to follow the petition’s progress.

[Our petitions and letters: the overall picture and next steps](/publikacje/odpowiedzi-na-petycje-wrzesien-2026/).
