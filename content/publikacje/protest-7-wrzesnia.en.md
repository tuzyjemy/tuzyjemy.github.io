The date is set. We meet on **Monday 7 September at 16:30** outside the Office for
Foreigners of the Pomeranian Voivodeship Office, ul. Chmielna 74/76 in Gdańsk.
The protest runs until 18:00.

We are demanding efficient residence legalisation proceedings, realistic deadlines and
the restoration of protection against excessive delay.

The full list of what we are demanding: [protest demands](/publikacje/postulaty-protestu-7-wrzesnia-2026/).

The assembly has been notified and appears in the [register of public assemblies of the Gdańsk City Office](https://bip.gdansk.pl/urzad-miejski/zgromadzenia-publiczne/Data-07-09-2026r-Czas-trwania-16-30-18-00,a,317325). The protest is lawful.

## Confirm you're coming

The sooner we know how many people to expect, the better we can prepare — including
for safety and for registering the assembly.

[Confirm your attendance on the event page](https://mobilizon.fr/events/5e9e3d5d-90c4-409f-9e61-87de17584abe)

Only the number of confirmations is public — never the names of the people who sent them.

Before you come, please read the [rules for taking part in the assembly](/publikacje/protest-7-wrzesnia/#zasady).

### Where to put it up

Anywhere people waiting for a decision are likely to be: at work, in an Eastern European
grocery, at a clinic, on campus, in a language school, at the hairdresser's, in a service
point. If you run a venue or a group — put one up and tell others.

The leaflet carries this site's address, our e-mail and Telegram, so anyone who sees it
will find the [demands](/publikacje/postulaty-protestu-7-wrzesnia-2026/) and the [rules of participation](/publikacje/protest-7-wrzesnia/#zasady).

## Rules for taking part in the assembly {#zasady}

We want everyone to feel safe and to be treated with respect.

- **No flags.** We do not bring flags of any kind — state, national, regional, party or organisational.
- **No hatred or discrimination.** We respect everyone regardless of origin, nationality, language, religion, gender or any other characteristic.
- **Everyone may speak their own language.** Please prepare the main text of banners in Polish, or add a Polish translation.
- **We act peacefully.** No aggression, threats, provocation or insults directed at others.
- **We do not bring weapons**, pyrotechnics or other dangerous objects.
- **We do not consume alcohol or intoxicants**, and we do not take part in the assembly under their influence.
- **We look after safety.** We do not block entrances, passageways or the road, and we follow the instructions of the organisers and the emergency services.

Anyone breaking these rules may be asked to stop the behaviour in question or to leave the assembly.
