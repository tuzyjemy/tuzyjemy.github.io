We were deeply saddened to learn of the tragedy that took place on 7 November 2026 in Kraków’s Main Market Square.

We offer our heartfelt condolences to the families and loved ones of those who lost their lives. Our thoughts are with you as you grieve.

We wish those injured a full recovery. We extend our support and solidarity to everyone who lived through these events — those harmed, witnesses and their loved ones.

We thank the police officers, paramedics, hospital staff and everyone who helped those affected.

Our thoughts are with the people of Kraków and everyone touched by this tragedy. In these difficult days, let us be there for one another, with care and respect for each other’s grief.

**The Tu Żyjemy team**
