We handed the petition to the first vice-voivode, Emil Rojek, on 7 September 2026, during
the [protest outside the Pomeranian Voivodeship Office](/publikacje/protest-7-09-2026-rezultaty/).

## What it contains

**11 points** from [the protest demands](/publikacje/postulaty-protestu-7-wrzesnia-2026/) — those falling within the competence
of the Voivode and the office: a backlog-reduction plan together with the underlying data,
the organisation of proceedings, related family cases, continued work after a complaint to
the WSA, and the timely issuing of certificates and handling of correspondence.

The remaining **4 demands** are not part of this petition — they concern the repeal of
art. 100d, legislative changes and digitalisation, and are addressed to the government and
the Sejm rather than the Voivode.

## Status

The petition is awaiting consideration. Under the petitions act a reply should be given
without undue delay and no later than **three months from filing** — that is, by
**7 December 2026**. The office has not yet given a case number.

During the protest the vice-voivode announced 24 new posts in the department for foreigners
and a working meeting was agreed. Those are spoken announcements, not a reply to the
petition — we will follow them up separately.

Once a reply arrives we will publish it under [institutional responses](/publikacje/) and
update this entry.
