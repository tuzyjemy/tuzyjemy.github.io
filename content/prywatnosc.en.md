tuzyjemy.pl does not use cookies, does not store anything in your browser and does not show ads. We don't ask for consent to tracking, because we don't track anyone.

## Visit statistics

To know which materials are read and in which languages, we use [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) — an open-source statistics tool that does not use cookies and does not store IP addresses.

For each page view, GoatCounter sees:

- the address of the page visited,
- the site the visitor came from (e.g. Telegram or a search engine),
- the type of browser and operating system, and the screen size,
- the approximate country, determined from the IP address — the address itself is not stored.

We also count clicks on downloadable files (e.g. PDFs) and on links leading off the site, for example to Telegram. We only see totals — we cannot tell who visited the site or what any particular person read.

Details: [GoatCounter privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).

If you block statistics scripts (for example with an ad blocker), the site works just the same.

## Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any server, GitHub receives technical information about the connection when a page is displayed, including the IP address — under [GitHub's own privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We have no access to this data.

Fonts and all other parts of the site load from our own domain, not from third-party servers.

## External links and translations

The site contains links to other services — public authorities, media, Telegram, Google Translate. Once you follow them, that service's privacy rules apply. "Translate" links open the page through Google Translate, so Google sees that connection.

## Contact

If you write to us by e-mail or on Telegram, we see what you send and use it only to reply and for our joint activities. Privacy questions: [contact@tuzyjemy.pl](mailto:contact@tuzyjemy.pl).
