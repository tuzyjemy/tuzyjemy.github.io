Anyone can submit a petition - an individual, an informal group, an organization - too
a person without Polish citizenship ([Article 2 of the Petitions Act](https://lexlege.pl/petycje/art-2/)).
The recipient has 3 months to consider; in the event of obstacles beyond its control, it may
extend this deadline, but not more than by another 3 months
([Article 10 of the Petitions Act](https://lexlege.pl/petycje/art-10/)).
