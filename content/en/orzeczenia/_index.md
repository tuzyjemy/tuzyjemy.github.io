We collect rulings that **actually help** — ones you can rely on in a reminder, a complaint
or an appeal. For each we give a one-sentence holding and the situation where it is useful.

Full texts are in the [Central Database of Administrative Court Rulings](https://orzeczenia.nsa.gov.pl/).
