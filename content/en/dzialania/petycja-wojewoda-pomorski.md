Petition submitted on June 24, 2026 to the Pomeranian Voivodeship Office. In the register
office, its subject was described as *"proposals regarding the systemic organization of the process
considering cases regarding the legalization of the stay of foreigners in the voivodeship
Pomeranian"*.

## Status

The office set the deadline for completing the case as **September 24, 2026** Until then
the petition remains ongoing - no response yet.


{{< notatka typ="info" >}}
This is one of **three documents submitted on June 24, 2026.** as a common package of actions to improve the legalization of stay. The initiative was preceded by a public collection of signatures. [Package Summary](https://t.me/tu_zyjemy/513/516)
