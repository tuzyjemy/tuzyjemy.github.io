We created a public database of judgments concerning inactivity and excessive delays in residence
proceedings. We analyzed more than 4,000 WSA and NSA judgments.

The analysis showed a clear change in practice: from October 2025, the prevailing line became
consistent with NSA case law, according to which art. 100d does not automatically prevent a finding
of inactivity or excessive delay. In our database, **96% of cases examined on the merits from that
point onward** ended favorably for the applicants.
