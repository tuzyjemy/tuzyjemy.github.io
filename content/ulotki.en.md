Each leaflet fits on a single A4 page and is entirely in one language — pick the one
spoken by the people you want to reach.

The **colour** version looks better on screen and in colour printing. The
**black-and-white** one is made for cheap printing and photocopiers.

{{< ulotki >}}

## Where to put it up

Anywhere people waiting for a decision are likely to be: at work, in an Eastern European
grocery, at a clinic, on campus, in a language school, at the hairdresser's, in a service
point. If you run a venue or a group — put one up and tell others.

The leaflet carries this site's address, our e-mail and Telegram, so anyone who sees it
will find the [demands](/postulaty/) and the [rules of participation](/zasady/).
