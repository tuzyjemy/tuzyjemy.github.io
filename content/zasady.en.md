We want everyone to feel safe and to be treated with respect.

- **No flags.** We do not bring flags of any kind — state, national, regional, party or organisational.
- **No hatred or discrimination.** We respect everyone regardless of origin, nationality, language, religion, gender or any other characteristic.
- **Everyone may speak their own language.** Please prepare the main text of banners in Polish, or add a Polish translation.
- **We act peacefully.** No aggression, threats, provocation or insults directed at others.
- **We do not bring weapons**, pyrotechnics or other dangerous objects.
- **We do not consume alcohol or intoxicants**, and we do not take part in the assembly under their influence.
- **We look after safety.** We do not block entrances, passageways or the road, and we follow the instructions of the organisers and the emergency services.

Anyone breaking these rules may be asked to stop the behaviour in question or to leave the assembly.
