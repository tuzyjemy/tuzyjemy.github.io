How to take part in Tu Żyjemy — as an individual, an informal group or an organisation.

We keep no membership lists and collect no fees. We work in tasks: someone writes a letter, someone translates, someone gathers data, someone stands with a banner.

What you can do

  • Collect data about your own case. Application date, decision date, authority — that is enough to feed the statistics .
  • Translate. The site runs in Polish, Russian, English, Ukrainian and Belarusian. Each new translation means dozens more people who will understand their rights.
  • Write and send letters. Use our templates — they are ready to go.
  • Document responses. Got a reply from an office? Send it to us and it will join the response archive .
